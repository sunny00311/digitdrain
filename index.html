CORE CAPABILITIES

Engineered for Urban Drainage Resilience

From street-level culverts to regional stormwater sumps, NaliSentry equips city planners and emergency crews with actionable spatial telemetry.

Interactive Drainage Map

Explore the citywide drainage grid with dynamic Leaflet.js mapping. Color-coded status pins pinpoint bottlenecks across catchments with popup telemetry.

Water-Level Monitoring

Continuous acoustic and ultrasonic depth estimation tracking water centimeters in real-time, showing capacity utilization and flow volume.

Critical Alert Visualization

Instantaneous hazard notices categorized by urgency. Operators can acknowledge alerts and trigger immediate focus on the live geospatial map.

Historical Water-Level Charts

Interactive Chart.js time-series plots revealing 24-hour surge curves, storm peak timing, and comparison against warning and critical safety lines.

Drainage Point Status

Comprehensive registry of every monitored sump and canal with real-time text search, status filters, depth capacity bars, and sensor battery metrics.

Maintenance Prioritization

Rank culverts and desilting interventions based on high risk and recurring warning trends to optimize municipal vacuum truck and pump deployment.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

How the Proposed System Operates

NaliSentry is designed around an end-to-end edge-to-cloud telemetry workflow.

01

Sensors Measure Water

Solar-powered ultrasonic and hydrostatic depth sensors deployed at drainage culverts measure water level fluctuations continuously.

02

Controller Collects Readings

An edge telemetry microcontroller buffers readings, logs battery status, and securely transmits packets via cellular or LPWAN networks.

03

Platform Evaluates Conditions

The telemetry engine ingests time-series data, evaluates water levels against dynamic thresholds, and flags rising trends.

04

Alerts Identify Hotspots

When thresholds are breached, visual alerts populate the central dashboard enabling emergency managers to dispatch desilting crews.
